## The challenge

Coordinating nonprofit-style operations means tracking activities that are often managed separately: campaigns, resources, volunteers and contribution entries. HopeLink brings those activities together in a single demonstration application.

## What I built

A responsive **Python/Flask** application backed by **SQLite**, using shared Jinja templates and custom HTML/CSS/JavaScript. The app includes a public overview, campaign views, a volunteer registration flow, resource inventory and an administrative dashboard.

Protected administrative actions, server-side validation, CSRF handling and route/workflow smoke tests illustrate practical backend engineering choices.

## Core workflows

- Manage sample campaigns and progress information.
- Register volunteers and record their skills or availability.
- Track example resources and inventory stock.
- Record simulated pledges and review summary information.
- Navigate a responsive dashboard designed for desktop and mobile.

## Outcome and honest scope

HopeLink is a **portfolio simulation**, not the website of a real operational NGO. Seeded identities, contributions and campaigns are fictional. The pledge flow records sample entries only—**it does not accept payments or verify donations**. No live hosted backend is claimed.
