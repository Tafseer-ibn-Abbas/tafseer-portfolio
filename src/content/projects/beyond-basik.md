## The brief

Beyond Basik Income Tax needed a polished, approachable web presence that communicates its tax preparation services and makes it easy for prospective customers to find the right information and contact the business.

## The solution

I developed a responsive six-page website using semantic HTML, modern CSS and vanilla JavaScript. The site is structured around **Home, Services, How It Works, Why Us, Reviews and Contact**, with shared navigation and a consistent visual identity based on client-provided brand assets.

The experience prioritizes direct paths to relevant services over unnecessary interface complexity. Dedicated contact routes, business/location information and a Google Reviews link support customer discovery.

## Engineering focus

- Responsive layouts and navigation from desktop to mobile.
- Consistent reusable styling and straightforward JavaScript interactions.
- Accessibility details such as keyboard-visible focus, descriptive interactive controls and reduced-motion support.
- Search and social metadata, canonical URLs and structured business information.
- Deployment with Vercel and a GitHub-connected source repository.

## Result and scope

A public-facing informational website for a real client. It is **not** a tax-filing portal, payment system or document-handling application. No unverified traffic, conversion or revenue claims are made.

The live URL is supplied in the project's public README; the exact availability of an external website can change independently of this portfolio.
